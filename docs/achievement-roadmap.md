# Achievement roadmap

Benster launches with 40 achievements. A future update should expand the
catalogue to 100 total achievements, adding the 60 non-holiday achievements
below. Thresholds are product proposals and should be calibrated against
launch-player data before implementation.

## Implementation notes

- Keep the launch 25 approachable; the expansion supplies longer-term goals.
- “Personal best” achievements should require a prior comparable completed
  sprint and a minimum answer count, so a trivial early score cannot unlock
  them.
- Define “available math mode” from the modes enabled in that app version.
- Track new metrics explicitly before surfacing achievements that need them:
  consecutive correct-answer streaks, daily/weekly aggregates, achievement
  unlock counts, and repeat comeback events.
- Keep all achievements optional, local-device only, and non-holiday-specific.
