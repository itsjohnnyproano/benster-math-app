# Benster achievement badges

This is the app-owned achievement pack: 40 transparent 512px PNGs plus `manifest.json`.
It does not depend on a Figma component library or Figma AI credits.

## Usage

Use `manifest.json` to map an achievement id to its local PNG. Keep the image
aspect ratio with a `contain` fit mode when displaying it.

## Future badges

Use this art direction for every new asset:

> One centered Benster achievement medal on a transparent background. Premium
> modern mobile-game collectible; chunky six-sided hexagonal shield, deeply
> inset jewel center, thick glossy beveled metal rim, dramatic but refined 3D
> lighting. Match the existing palette and scale. Use Benster, the black and
> white penguin with the purple lightning headband, only when personality makes
> the achievement clearer; use a strong symbol for performance or math skills.
> No captions, surrounding UI, watermark, checkerboard, or extra text.

Prefer Benster for milestone, momentum, exploration, comeback, and celebration
badges. Keep precision and math-skill badges symbol-forward.
